About

Conteur was made for a particular kind of attention — the kind that crosses time zones for an exhibition, that reads a city through its literature before landing, that moves through neighbourhoods at the pace of looking rather than ticking.

We curate what is worth your time: the shows drawing people across borders and the ones that haven't been found yet, the books that make a place legible, and the itineraries written for people who want to see rather than sightsee.

Each city on the board is chosen deliberately. New ones are added when they earn a place.
