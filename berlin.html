Berlin

The City in Print

Novel

Goodbye to Berlin

Christopher Isherwood

The novel that became Cabaret. Isherwood's Berlin is decadent, hungry, and on the edge of catastrophe — written from inside the Weimar Republic's last gasps. Still the definitive portrait of the city's capacity for transformation.

History

Berlin: The Downfall 1945

Antony Beevor

A visceral account of Berlin's final months in WWII. Essential context for understanding the city's relationship with its own ruins — and why rebuilding, in Berlin, is never just about architecture.

Cultural Essay

The Naked Eye

Yoko Tawada

A Vietnamese woman stranded in Berlin, narrating her dislocation through the lens of Catherine Deneuve's films. Tawada's Berlin is a city of beautiful misunderstandings — essential for the contemporary art crowd.
