Mexico City

The City in Literature

Novel

Pedro Páramo

Juan Rulfo

The novel García Márquez credited with teaching him to write. A man travels to a ghost town to find his father and finds the dead still talking. Only 124 pages — every one of them a different gravity from anything before it in Spanish literature.

Essays

Tell Me How It Ends

Valeria Luiselli

Forty questions asked to child migrants at the US border, and the world that produced them. Luiselli writes CDMX and the border from the inside — lucid, furious, and impossible to put down. Read it before visiting.

Novel

The Savage Detectives

Roberto Bolaño

Two young poets scour Mexico City in pursuit of a vanished avant-garde movement. Bolaño's CDMX is noisy, erotic, and alive at 3am. The book that made Latin American literature matter again to the rest of the world.
