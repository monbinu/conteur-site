Munich

The City in Print

Novel

Munich

Robert Harris

Two diplomats — one British, one German — reunite during the 1938 Munich Agreement. Harris rebuilds the city's most consequential weekend with surgical precision. Impossible to put down, impossible not to think about after.

Novel

Steppenwolf

Hermann Hesse

Written partly in Munich, where Hesse lived during his most restless years. Harry Haller's crisis of identity — half-man, half-wolf, entirely alienated — remains the essential portrait of European intellectual dislocation.

Art History

The Blaue Reiter Almanac

Wassily Kandinsky & Franz Marc, eds.

Published in Munich in 1912, this is the foundational document of the movement that made the city an epicentre of abstraction. Read it before or after the Lenbachhaus — either way, it will change how you look.
