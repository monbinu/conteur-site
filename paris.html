Paris  ·  France

Paris

The City on the Page

Novel

Bel-Ami

Guy de Maupassant

A portrait of social ambition and moral corruption in Belle Époque Paris. Maupassant's city is seductive and pitiless in equal measure — still the most honest novel about the city's appetite.

Memoir

A Moveable Feast

Ernest Hemingway

Hemingway's Paris of the 1920s — cafés, poverty, Gertrude Stein, the races at Auteuil. Sentimental and sharp at once. Essential reading before your first visit, revisable before every return.

Art History

The Shock of the New

Robert Hughes

The definitive account of how modern art was born in Paris. Before visiting Pompidou or Orsay, read Hughes — it transforms what you'll see on the walls from objects into arguments.
