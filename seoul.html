Seoul

Voices from the Peninsula

Novel

The Vegetarian

Han Kang

Three interconnected novellas about a Seoul woman who stops eating meat — and the violence, beauty, and unravelling that follows. Han Kang's writing is visceral, precise, and unlike anything else in contemporary fiction.

Novel

Please Look After Mom

Kyung-sook Shin

A woman goes missing in the Seoul subway. Her children search for her and, in doing so, search for who she was. A devastating portrait of family, memory, and the invisible labour of mothers — and of a city always in motion.

Cultural Non-fiction

The Birth of Korean Cool

Euny Hong

How South Korea engineered a cultural renaissance — K-pop, K-drama, K-beauty, and the soft power that built it all. Essential context for understanding why the contemporary art scene in Seoul is as significant as any in the world right now.
